    Burlington G2 practice routes

    Unofficial. DriveTest doesn't publish routes, and examiners vary them. These are routes test-takers reported (turn-by-turn from 2018; the same areas are still named in 2022–2026 reports), snapped onto OpenStreetMap. Use them to learn the area, not to memorise one line.

    Set it up before you drive and mount the phone. In Ontario a driver may not hold or touch a phone while driving, even stopped at a red light. For a G1 or G2 driver, a first conviction means a 30-day suspension. Live GPS mode needs no taps; let the supervising driver handle anything else. ontario.ca

    How to

    • Phone: choose a route, tap Start drive, and drive. Checkpoints advance on their own. If it gets confused, the passenger can tap a checkpoint under All checkpoints to re-sync.
    • Laptop: Tap through mode. Use Next and Back, tap any checkpoint, or click the progress bar.
    • Install: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen (Android Chrome: menu → Install app).
